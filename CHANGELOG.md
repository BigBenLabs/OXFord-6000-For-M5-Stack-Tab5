# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-17

- Initial Tab5 FSRS vocabulary trainer.
- Three card modes: English to Chinese, Chinese to English, and dictation.
- FSRS-6 fixed default parameters and stability-based progression.
- A1 to A2 to B1 to B2 to C1 monotonic level unlocking.
- SQLite progress database with PERSIST journaling and optional review log.
- M5Stack Keyboard character-mode input over I2C.
- ES8388 UK/US pronunciation playback from the TF card.
- Dark green 1280x720 LVGL UI with Chinese and IPA font subset.
- ESP32-P4 v1.x compatibility, ST7121/ST7123 display support, FAT long names.
- `Ctrl+S` device screenshot capture to `/sdcard/screenshots/`.
